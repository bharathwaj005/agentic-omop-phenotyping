Neuro candidate freeze package. Twelve cohorts were retained after the v2 entry Jaccard gate. Excluded candidates and reasons are listed in exclusion_log.csv. Entry arms and concepts were read from tasks/reference_v2. patient_evaluable is true when at least one of 2694 Eunomia persons has an event matching a resolved entry concept; eunomia_entry_person_count is the distinct-person count across entry domains. This is an entry-event coverage check, not full cohort execution. Complexity and cohort names are from the existing benchmark pool and Phenotype Library metadata.
